## 1. Purpose
This contract aims to establish a clear framework for effective collaboration, accountability, and communication among team members, ensuring timely and high-quality completion of the project.

## 2. Team Roles & Responsibilities

### 2.1 Project Manager/Overseer
- **Leadership & Oversight**: Provide guidance, delegate tasks, and oversee project progress.
- **Timeline Management**: Ensure adherence to deadlines and timely submission of deliverables.
- **Help members and team leads**: Make regular commits to fix code and documentation and help the members and team leads if there is some problem in code/documentation/testing/integration/frontend/backend/network, etc.
- **Conflict Resolution**: Mediate disagreements and foster a collaborative team environment.
- **Meeting Coordination**: Schedule and lead regular team meetings, set agendas, and follow up on action items.

### 2.2 Team Leads
- **Leadership**: Provide guidance to members and hold meetings, delegate tasks, and oversee integration.
- **Timeline Management**: Ensure adherence to deadlines and timely sub
- **Meeting Coordination**: Schedule and lead regular sub team meetings, set agendas, and follow up on action items.
- **Meet with Project Overseer**: Discuss problems and integration methods. Resolve problems and brainstorm solutions
- **Advise Project Manager**: Give proper feedback and help the project manager if there is something that was missed

### 2.3 Team Members
Each team member will assume specific technical roles (e.g., Backend Developer, Frontend Developer, Testers, Test Writers) and is expected to:
- **Deliver Assigned Tasks**: Complete tasks as assigned, adhering to quality standards.
- **Collaborate Proactively**: Share updates, assist others, and actively engage in group discussions.
- **Attend Meetings**: Participate in all scheduled meetings and communicate conflicts in advance.
- **Provide Feedback**: Contribute constructive feedback to peers and accept feedback professionally.

## 3. Meeting Protocols

- **Frequency**: Weekly meetings via [platform, e.g., Zoom or in-person].
- **Attendance**: Required unless a valid reason is communicated at least 24 hours in advance.
- **Preparation**: Review agenda and come prepared to discuss current tasks and challenges.
- **Minutes**: The designated team member records and shares minutes post-meeting.

## 4. Communication Standards

- **Primary Channels**: [Discord] for daily communication; all official updates will be shared in [Discord Channels].
- **Response Time**: Team members must respond within 24 hours to project-related messages.
- **Professionalism**: All communication should be respectful, concise, and relevant to the project.

## 5. Code and Documentation Standards

- **Version Control**: Use [GitLab] with a strict policy for branch management and merging.
- **Code Review**: Every PR (Pull Request) must be reviewed by at least one team member before merging.
- **Documentation**: All code must include clear comments and each module must have corresponding documentation.

## 6. Conflict Resolution

- **Initial Resolution**: Team members are encouraged to resolve conflicts respectfully among themselves.
- **Escalation**: If unresolved, the issue should be brought to the Project Manager/Overseer, who will mediate a solution.
- **Reporting Concerns**: Any team member may approach the Project Manager with confidential concerns without fear of retaliation.

## 7. Accountability and Consequences

- **Quality Standards**: Work not meeting project standards must be revised by the team leader before submission.
- **Remarks in Peer Review**: In extreme cases, the members will write about the poor performance.
- **Accountability Table:**

  | Name        | Adjustment | Reason                                                                   |
  |-------------|------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------|
  | Alpha       | 0 | Consistently met deadlines and team expectations.                        |
  | Beta        | -1 | Low Contribution. No inputs.                                             |
  | Gamma | -9 | No Response and No contact. |
## 8. Acknowledgment
By signing below, each team member agrees to the terms outlined above and commits to active, respectful, and professional collaboration.
